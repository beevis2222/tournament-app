Event

Describe the event

Type it the way you'd say it to your head pro, like “club championship, gross match play, three flights” or “54-hole net stroke play, flighted.” You can adjust any setting after.

Event settings

More settings

Most events never need these. They're set from the format automatically.

Handicap limits

Capped or lowered indexes are used for every handicap calculation. Flags show in the pre-event check.

Course and tees

Par . Each tee has its own rating and slope, and every player's course handicap uses the tee they play.

Hole pars and stroke index

Stroke index decides where handicap strokes fall, hardest hole = 1.

Field

Upload the spreadsheet you already keep, or copy the rows straight out of Excel or Google Sheets and paste them below. Name (or first and last), handicap index, and tee are picked up automatically. Include a group, hole or tee time column (or one row per foursome) and your groups become the pairings.

Pairings

Event day

Check players in, see which cards are still out and exactly what's missing, then finalize the results.

Print center

Cart signs, pairings, scorecards, results and flyers built straight from your event. Change a pairing or post a score and these pages update with it.

Club branding
Outing host

For outings, add the hosting company. Their logo prints next to the club's on every piece.

Live scores link and QR code

Add a link and a QR code prints on every cart sign and shows on the clubhouse TV, so players can scan it to follow the leaderboard. Once the full app is live, each event gets its own public link automatically. Until then you can use any web address, like your club's event page.

Fonts, colors and sizes

Sized for US Letter. If Print doesn't open here, download the print file and open it in any browser, then print or save as PDF.
